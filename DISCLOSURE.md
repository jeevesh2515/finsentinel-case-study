# Disclosure

## What this repository is

- A public documentation case study describing the engineering of a private product prototype.
- A demonstration of design reasoning: architecture, trust boundaries, human review, evaluation discipline, and honest limitation reporting.
- A portfolio artefact for professional review (recruitment, CV, and interview discussion).

## What this repository is not

- **Not the product.** The FinSentinel source code, prompts, schemas, scoring internals, retrieval logic, infrastructure configuration, and operational details remain private.
- **Not an open-source release.** No software licence for the product is granted anywhere in this repository. See [OWNERSHIP-AND-LICENSE.md](OWNERSHIP-AND-LICENSE.md).
- **Not a compliance or legal statement.** Nothing here is legal advice, compliance certification, or a regulatory position.
- **Not evidence of customer deployment.** No customer, pilot, or production usage exists.

## Data and examples

- Every example in [examples/](examples/synthetic-transaction.json) is fabricated for illustration and marked as synthetic.
- [assets/demo-poster.svg](assets/demo-poster.svg) is a hand-drawn mock interface, not a screenshot of any real system.
- An interactive public showcase is available at [https://finsentinel-chi.vercel.app](https://finsentinel-chi.vercel.app). It operates entirely client-side with synthetic fixture data and does not connect to any private backend or store customer data.
- No customer names, records, contact details, transaction data, alerts, case material, or personal data appear in this repository.
- No credentials, secrets, connection strings, or configuration values are included or implied.

## Deliberate exclusions

Private repository links, internal hostnames, deployment URLs, account identifiers, database names, and internal documents are intentionally excluded. Internal commit identifiers are omitted from the evaluation records; phase-gate and date identifiers are used instead, because the source repository is private.

## Provenance and third-party material

- All diagrams, examples, and text in this repository were written specifically for public release.
- FinSentinel was developed as a personal project; provenance notes for the original pre-implementation brainstorm remain in the private repository. No employer, client, or university material is included, and no third-party confidential material is reproduced.
- Regulatory references, where mentioned, are described at a generic level; no copyrighted regulatory text is reproduced here.
